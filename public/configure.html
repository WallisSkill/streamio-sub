SubtitleCat cho Stremio

Chon ngon ngu uu tien roi bam Cai vao Stremio. Mac dinh la Tieng Viet.

1. Ngon ngu uu tien

Thu tu chon = thu tu uu tien hien thi trong Stremio.

2. Nguon phu de

Bat cang nhieu nguon cang de tim ra sub. Ket qua tu moi nguon deu bi loc lai theo dung ten phim/tap truoc khi tra ve Stremio.

3. Xem hop phap o dau

Hien them muc "Xem tren ..." trong tab Streams cua phim, bam vao se mo app/trang cua nen tang do – dung khi ban muon xem ban long tieng chinh chu. Bo chon het la tat han.

4. Tuy chon

De mac dinh neu ban khong chac.

5. Cai dat

Cai vao Stremio Cai qua Stremio Web Xem manifest.json
Phu de lay tu nhieu nguon roi phuc vu lai qua /sub/<token>.srt (tu dong giai nen .zip va chuyen ve UTF-8).
API key (neu nhap) nam trong link cai dat – dung chia se link nay cho nguoi khac.
Neu deploy len domain that, dat bien moi truong PUBLIC_URL=https://ten-mien-cua-ban.
